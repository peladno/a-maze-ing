## 4. Learned / 学んだこと

- **An independent loop is one that other loops cannot be combined into**; each wall opened on a tree adds exactly
  one.
- **Separate what never changes from what depends on the size:** the "42" offsets are a module constant, the
  position is computed per generator.
- **`frozenset` has no `add`**: build a `set`, then `frozenset(cells)` makes a new, frozen one.
- **flake8 passing, mypy passing and the tests passing are three different claims**, and none of them is "it is
  correct"; today's bugs got past each of them at least once.
